## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
Serve what the calendar needs to show the phase: the flagged entries of the whole window (the counter and markers), the recent silent auto-adjusts (the origin icon's note), and the D-03 resurfacing of a started meeting that moved, with the entry it was logged on.

Purpose: OSYNC-01 (resurface + "move my entry here" data), OSYNC-02 (flag list + counter data), OSYNC-03 (data behind the review popover), with D-10, D-14 and D-19 scoping.
Output: `OutOfSyncFlags` read model, `MeetingHints::movedFrom`, `moved_from` in the resource, the read wiring, a new read test class and the updated shape assertions.
</objective>
