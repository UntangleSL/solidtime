## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The Graph half of "removed": a direct lookup of one linked occurrence, mapped to a small result type that callers turn into observations. It is the only safe way to tell a removed or declined meeting (404 ErrorItemNotFound) from one moved out of the window (200 at a new time), because both are simply absent from a window (RESEARCH anti-pattern, Phase 1 decision document). This plan also adds the phase's tunable constants to the fork config.

Purpose: the D-07 "removed only on a successful lookup that answers 404" rule and the D-11 budget, ready for the interactive sync (06-09) and the daily job (06-15).
Output: `EventLookupStatus`, `EventLookup`, `GraphCalendarClient::event()`, the `out_of_sync` config block and a local unit test.
</objective>
