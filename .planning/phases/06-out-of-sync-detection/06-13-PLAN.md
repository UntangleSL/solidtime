## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The D-03 experience on the meeting side: a meeting that Outlook moved after it had started shows again as a normal suggestion at its new slot, tells the user it was already logged, and offers "Move my entry here" beside the default "Create time entry".

Purpose: OSYNC-01 "it shows again at its new slot, where the user can create a second entry or move the existing one there".
Output: `OutlookMovedMeeting.vue` and the extended ghost detail, hover card and ghost layer, with Vitest coverage.
</objective>
