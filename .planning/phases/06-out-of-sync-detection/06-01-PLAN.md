## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
Tracer of the phase: the thinnest backend path from an Outlook change to a time entry, end to end. A meeting that has not started yet is moved in Outlook, the next calendar sync sees it at its new slot, the pure rules decide "auto adjust", and the entry follows through core's own update path. The same plan lays the schema for every later plan (all link state columns, the partial unique index), proves the full rules matrix locally, and aligns the project docs with the owner's D-12 exception.

Purpose: OSYNC-02 first bullet (auto adjust) end to end, and the foundations of OSYNC-01/02 (rules, snapshot, link state). Later plans add lookups for absent meetings (06-05), applier safety (06-04), the read (06-06), the actions (06-08) and the daily job (06-09).
Output: one migration, the `App\Microsoft365\OutOfSync` namespace (value objects, rules, scope, sightings, applier), `CoreTimeEntryUpdater`, the sync hook, a feature test (CI) and a rules unit test (local), docs aligned with D-12. Wave-1 CI anchor.
</objective>
