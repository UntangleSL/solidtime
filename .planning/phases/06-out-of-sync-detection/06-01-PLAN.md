**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

  2. Unknown -> Nothing (D-07)
  3. Present, not all-day, at the snapshot slot -> ClearFlag when flagged (D-17), else Nothing (user edits never flag, D-07)

  5. not started: (a) Cancelled -> Flag cancelled (Nothing when already flagged cancelled); (b) Removed -> Flag removed (Nothing when already flagged removed); (c) Present all-day -> Flag moved with the seen times and allDay true, never AutoAdjust (D-18; Nothing when identical to the current flag); (d) Present, entry at the snapshot -> AutoAdjust(seen) (D-04); (e) Present, entry already at the seen slot -> Resync(seen); (f) Present otherwise -> Flag moved with the seen times (D-04; Nothing when identical to the current flag)
