## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
Complete the interactive-sync side of the detector: confirm absent linked meetings with lookups (the only safe signal for "removed"), prove every sync case of the rules on real Graph-shaped answers, and report how many entries a sync moved.

Purpose: OSYNC-01 "checked on calendar sync" and OSYNC-02 (moved, edited, cancelled, removed, never on errors) through the path users trigger when they open a week.
Output: `Observation::fromLookup()`, `OutOfSyncLookups` (shared with the daily job in 06-09), the lookup step in `OutlookMeetingSyncService`, `SyncOutcome.auto_adjusted`, and the sync feature matrix.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-15 | Tampering (integrity) | False "removed" flags | high | mitigate | Lookup confirmation for every absent id; only 404 ErrorItemNotFound counts; partial/failed windows skip lookups (tests) |
| T-06-16 | Denial of service (self) | Lookup budget per sync | medium | mitigate | At most 5 lookups, inside the 15 s deadline, stop on throttle / reconnect / unknown, persisted back-off |
| T-06-17 | Information disclosure | Scrub of cancelled meetings | medium | mitigate | Sightings are taken before the scrub but keep ids/times only; the scrub of subject/attendees still runs (test) |
| T-06-18 | Tampering | Concurrent syncs of one user | low | mitigate | Lookups and apply run inside the existing per-user sync lock plus the applier's own lock |
| T-06-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo/Composer installs | high | mitigate | No package installs |
</threat_model>
