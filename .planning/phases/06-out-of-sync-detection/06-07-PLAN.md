## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The client brain of the phase: one context API that every Phase 6 component uses. It holds the flags and auto-adjusts from the read, runs the resolve actions (keep, adjust, delete) and the D-03 move with the UI-SPEC feedback, opens the review panel, jumps to the first flagged entry, announces silent adjusts after a watched sync, and lets browser tests drive all of it through the test hook.

Purpose: OSYNC-01..03 behaviour on the client; components in wave 3 only render and emit.
Output: extended `useOutlookMeetings.ts` and its tests.
</objective>
