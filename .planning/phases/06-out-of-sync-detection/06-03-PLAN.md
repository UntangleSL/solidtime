## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

Also the contrast rows for the new amber marker and counter icon.

Purpose: OSYNC-02/03 copy and data contracts on the client, independent of the server plans (field names fixed here and in 06-06 / 06-08).
Output: lang keys, types, API parsing and calls, `syncFlagReason.ts`, `describeBlock.ts`, Vitest and contrast coverage.
</objective>
