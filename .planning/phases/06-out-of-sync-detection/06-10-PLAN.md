## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

Output: `OutlookSyncFlagMarker`, `OutlookEntryMenuItems`, the extended `OutlookEntryBadge` and `OutlookMeetingContextItems`, Vitest coverage. Mounting into core slots happens in 06-14.
</objective>
