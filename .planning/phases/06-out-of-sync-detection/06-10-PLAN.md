## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The user's choices on the server: keep a flagged entry as it is, adjust it to Outlook, or (for a started meeting that moved) move the existing entry to the new slot instead of creating a second one. Delete stays core's own endpoint.

Purpose: OSYNC-03 (keep / adjust / delete) and the D-03 half of OSYNC-01 ("create a second entry or move the existing one there").
Output: `OutOfSyncController`, three routes, active-only `linkOf()`, calendar prop URLs, two new feature test classes and the updated route count / prop shape.
</objective>
