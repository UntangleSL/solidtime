## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The daily background check (D-11): a scheduled command fans out one queued job per connected user; each job looks up that user's linked meetings in the window and applies the same rules as the interactive sync. The compose file gets the Microsoft variables on the queue and scheduler services so the job can refresh tokens in production.

Purpose: OSYNC-01 "and by a daily background job"; keeps the counter (OSYNC-02) right for weeks the user has not opened.
Output: command, job, schedule registration from the fork provider, compose environment, two CI feature test classes.
</objective>
