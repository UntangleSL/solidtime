## Phase Goal

**As a** team member who already logged a meeting, **I want to** be told when Outlook moves or cancels it and choose what happens to my entry, **so that** my logged hours are never changed behind my back.

<objective>
The one place where the user decides about a flagged entry: a popover with what changed and the choices Keep as is, Adjust to Outlook and Delete entry (two-step), with the loading, failure, stale and focus rules of the UI-SPEC.

Purpose: OSYNC-03 in the UI and the visible half of OSYNC-02 (what changed, old -> new).
Output: `OutlookSyncFlagPanel.vue` with Vitest coverage; 06-14 mounts it once in `Calendar.vue`.
</objective>
