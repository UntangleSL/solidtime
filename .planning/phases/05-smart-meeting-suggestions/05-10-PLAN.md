## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** have recurring meetings come already assigned to the project I used last time, and other meetings come with a good project suggestion learned from my history, **so that** logging meeting time is mostly confirming instead of choosing.

<objective>
The lane and the detail panel of Phase 5: pre-assigned meetings show their project in the block, the panel explains the pre-assignment and confirms it in one click, More actions carries the series controls, and occurrences of ignored series appear in the faded variant when dismissed meetings are shown.

Purpose: RULE-01 (pre-assigned look, D-03), RULE-02 (confirm from the panel), RULE-04/05 (series controls and the why line, D-10, D-11), UI-SPEC sections 1, 3 and 5.
Output: ghost layer, ghost detail and meeting summary deltas with Vitest coverage, dot-ring contrast rows.
</objective>
