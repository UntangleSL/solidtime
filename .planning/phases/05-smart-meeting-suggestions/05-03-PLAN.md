## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** have recurring meetings come already assigned to the project I used last time, and other meetings come with a good project suggestion learned from my history, **so that** logging meeting time is mostly confirming instead of choosing.

<objective>
Bring the attendee data the learned suggestions need into the meeting cache, and nowhere else (D-09): Graph `$select` gains `attendees`; the sync stores the organizer address and a capped, minimal attendee list for the meeting owner; the scrub and Disconnect remove them with the rest of the meeting; the read never shows them. A small adapter turns a cached meeting and the user into the input of 05-02's pure signal extractor.

Purpose: RULE-03 data and ROADMAP criterion 5 (attendee data stored only for the owner and purged with the meeting data). 05-05 (capture at conversion) and 05-09 (scoring in the read) consume `MeetingSignalInput`.
Output: one migration, sync and client changes, adapter, PHPUnit coverage, DECISIONS-graph.md amendment.
</objective>
