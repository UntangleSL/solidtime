## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** have recurring meetings come already assigned to the project I used last time, and other meetings come with a good project suggestion learned from my history, **so that** logging meeting time is mostly confirming instead of choosing.

Every UI plan of waves 2 and 3 builds on these without touching them.

Purpose: RULE-01 (prefill from the series), RULE-03 (prefill from a confident suggestion, alternatives), RULE-05 (the reason line), D-07, D-13, D-16 on the client.
Output: lang keys (46 per language), types, two pure modules with Vitest suites, conversionApi additions, the shared OutlookProjectDot component, label-count test update.
</objective>
