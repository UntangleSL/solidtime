## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want to** have recurring meetings come already assigned to the project I used last time, and other meetings come with a good project suggestion learned from my history, **so that** logging meeting time is mostly confirming instead of choosing.

<objective>
The mini form becomes mostly a confirmation: it opens with the series pre-assignment or a confident learned suggestion, says why, offers the next alternatives as one-click chips, warns how the series will follow the choice, and reports how it was prefilled.

Purpose: RULE-01 (form prefilled from the series), RULE-03 (prefill + alternatives), RULE-05 (see why), D-05, D-07, D-08, D-16 on the client.
Output: OutlookSuggestionPicks component and the OutlookConvertForm delta (single and batch), with Vitest coverage.
</objective>
