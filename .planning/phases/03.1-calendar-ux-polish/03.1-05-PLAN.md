## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want** a calendar that is easier to read and move around (view options, readable meeting blocks, hover details, smooth zoom and scroll), **so that** working with time entries and Outlook meetings feels as fluid as in Clockify.

<objective>
UX-03 / D-05: a hover card with the meeting's details on desktop pointers (03.1-UI-SPEC section 2), built on reka `HoverCard` and sharing one summary component with the existing click panel so Phase 4 adds its action once; plus the fork half of UX-04 / D-06: the layer closes hover cards and panels before the context menu mounts and keeps them closed while it is open (03.1-RESEARCH Pattern 4, Pattern 5, Pitfalls 4-6).

Purpose: meeting details at a glance without a click, and no tooltip or panel can ever sit over the right-click menu.
Output: shared summary, hover card wrapper, delay helper, layer wiring and gating, Vitest proof (browser proof in 03.1-07).
</objective>
