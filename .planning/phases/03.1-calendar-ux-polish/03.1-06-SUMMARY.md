# Phase 3.1 Plan 06: Wheel / pinch zoom on the Grid Scale ladder Summary

Ctrl/Cmd + wheel and trackpad pinch over the calendar now zoom one Grid Scale step at a time (25/50/100/150/300 %), keeping the time under the pointer where it was. A plain wheel still scrolls natively. Two zoom buttons sit in the header corner, a "Zoom 150%" pill confirms each change, and zooming no longer refetches time entries.

## What was built

- **`calendarZoom.ts`** (new core module, pure): the ladder `ZOOM_SLOT_MINUTES = [60, 30, 15, 10, 5]`, `zoomPercent` (1500 / slotMinutes), `nextZoomSlotMinutes` / `canZoom` (an off-ladder value snaps to the nearest step first, and the ends clamp), `normalizeWheelDelta` (lines x40, pages x800), `createWheelZoomAccumulator` (48 px threshold, at most one step per 100 ms, reset after 150 ms idle or on a direction change, negative delta zooms in), `anchoredScrollTop` (never below 0), and `datesAffectingSettingsKey` (every setting except slotMinutes; showWeekends normalised).
- **`useWheelZoom.ts`** (new core composable): non-passive `wheel`, `gesturestart` and `gesturechange` listeners on the `.fc` root, which covers the grid, the time axis and the sticky header. Ctrl/Cmd wheels are always `preventDefault`-ed. They step only when no drag, resize or selection is in progress. `stepZoom` reads the anchor time before the settings write, does one settings write per step, then sets `scrollTop` after `nextTick`. `zoomIn()` / `zoomOut()` anchor at the scroller's vertical centre. The 1200 ms indicator state restarts on each step and is cleared on dispose. Safari pinch steps once per scale ratio of at least 1.25 or at most 0.8, then re-baselines.
- **`CalendarZoomControls.vue`** (new core component): two stacked 24 px ghost buttons (`size-6 p-0`, 12 px Plus/Minus icons, 2 px gap) with aria-labels "Zoom in" / "Zoom out". At the ends of the ladder they get `aria-disabled="true"`, stay focusable and emit nothing. The tooltips (300 ms) read "Zoom in (Ctrl + scroll)", or ⌘ on macOS.
- **`TimeEntryCalendar.vue`** (core, additive): calls `useWheelZoom` with the interacting guard and renders the controls in the formerly empty header corner. It adds `relative` to `.fc` and a persistent zoom pill (`role=status`, `aria-live=polite`, `pointer-events-none`, `absolute left-14 z-20`, top = measured header height + 8 px). The deep settings watch became `watch(() => datesAffectingSettingsKey(calendarSettings.value), emitDatesChange)`.
- **e2e:** a `Calendar view options: zoom` describe with 8 tests. They cover: ctrl wheel without page zoom plus the pill; a real Control + wheel (Chromium only); anchoring; plain wheel scrolling; buttons and the ladder ends plus the popover's "1 hour"; no `/time-entries` requests while zooming; selection snapping at 300%; and the hover card closing on a zoom step.
- **Ledger:** the TimeEntryCalendar.vue row is extended and the three new file pairs are listed in "New files inside core directories". These edits are in the Phase 3.1 area, not at the end of the file.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Orchestrator rule] No push, no Gate W.** The executor rules for this run say "Do NOT push" because the orchestrator merges the worktree branch. The plan's push protocol, CI wait and Gate W were therefore not run here, and the fast workflows and the Playwright zoom specs will run in CI after the orchestrator merges and pushes.
2. **[Rule 3 - Test environment] happy-dom WheelEvent shim.** happy-dom's `WheelEvent` drops `ctrlKey`, `metaKey` and `clientY` from its init dict. `useWheelZoom.test.ts` defines them on the event before dispatching, and real browsers keep them, so the e2e helper does not need this. The harness also awaits one `nextTick` after mount, because vueuse attaches ref-target listeners in a post-flush watcher.
3. **[Rule 1 - Test reliability] e2e details.** The plan did not cover these, but the specs needed them to be reliable in CI:
   - The "anchored" test centres the 14:00 row in the scroller instead of scrolling it to the top. With the row at the top, the 30-minute level would clamp `scrollTop` at the grid's end.
   - Scripted wheel steps are spaced 200 ms apart because of the 100 ms rate limit.
   - The aria-disabled button is clicked with `force: true`, because Playwright treats `aria-disabled` as disabled.
   - The selection test checks the created entry's POST body (start 10:00 UTC, end on the 15-minute grid), following the existing calendar snap tests.
4. **Worktree setup.** The branch was reset to `feature/m365-integration` @ 712100f4. `node_modules` and `vendor` junctions point to the main checkout (both are gitignored, not committed).
