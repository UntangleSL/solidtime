# Phase 03.1 Plan 02: Sync cooldown fix Summary

The Sync button now cools down only after a manual sync or a server back-off. While it waits it shows a draining 16px ring, the label "Sync in 8s" and a tooltip plus `aria-describedby` hint that says why it is waiting. It uses `aria-disabled`, not the `disabled` attribute, so focus stays on the button after Enter. Automatic syncs from week navigation no longer leave the button unavailable after they finish (the UX-06 bug).

## What was built

- **Composable (`useOutlookMeetings.ts`)**: `extendCooldown(ms, kind)`. After a sync settles, it runs only when `target.force` is true (10 s, `manual`) or a back-off applies: a throttled or unavailable `retry_after`, or the synthetic unavailable answer from a 429 `Retry-After`. The longer of the two wins. The read `sync.retry_after` watcher starts a `backoff` cooldown. The context exposes `cooldownStartedAt` and `cooldownKind`. `canSync`, `requestSync`, the debounce, the freshness skip and single flight are unchanged.
- **Helpers (`syncStatus.ts`)**: `formatCooldownSeconds` ("8s", "1m", "1m 20s"), `remainingCooldownSeconds` (rounds up, never below 0) and `cooldownProgress` (from 1 down to 0, clamped).
- **Control (`OutlookCalendarControls.vue`)**:
  - Uses `aria-disabled="true"` while syncing or cooling, with `aria-disabled:` muted styling and `cursor-not-allowed`. The click handler returns early when Sync is unavailable.
  - Sets `data-cooldown="manual|backoff"` while cooling.
  - Shows the SVG ring in place of RefreshCw: a `text-border` track and a `text-text-tertiary` arc. The arc is rotated -90 degrees and drains counter-clockwise, with a `motion-safe:` 1 s linear transition.
  - At md and up the label reads "Sync in 8s" with `tabular-nums`; the button has `md:min-w-[8rem]`.
  - The first tooltip line is the hint for the cooldown kind, followed by the existing status lines.
  - An sr-only hint span, linked through `aria-describedby` only while cooling, is not a live region. The countdown never enters `role="status"`.
- **Copy**: the six phase keys in en, mirrored in es exactly as in UI-SPEC; `OutlookCalendarLabels` is extended. The calendar labels prop now has 40 keys (en and es each have 40).
- **Tests**:
  - Real-composable cases: an automatic sync leaves Sync available; manual 10 s cooldown with focus kept, the ring, and a second click posting nothing; an automatic throttled sync backs off 20 s; a read `retry_after` gives a 15 s backoff; manual + unavailable `retry_after` 3 still cools 10 s; "Sync in 10s" then "Sync in 8s"; tooltip text for manual and backoff (80 s shows as "1m 20s"); describedby versus the live region.
  - Fake-context cases: clicks are ignored while unavailable; the ticker stops when the cooldown ends and on unmount (`vi.getTimerCount`).
  - A cooldown-helpers block.
  - Existing assertions on the `disabled` attribute now check `aria-disabled`.
