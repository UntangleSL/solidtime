# Phase 3.1 Plan 05: Outlook meeting hover card and context-menu layering Summary

Meeting details appear in a reka HoverCard after a 400 ms mouse or pen rest. The card and the click panel render their rows through one `OutlookMeetingSummary`, and the layer closes every card and panel before the calendar context menu opens.

## What was built

- **`hoverCardDelay.ts`**: `HOVER_OPEN_DELAY_MS` 400, `HOVER_CLOSE_DELAY_MS` 120, `HOVER_INSTANT_WINDOW_MS` 300. Module-level state is shared across day columns. `hoverOpenDelay` (computed) returns 0 while a card is open and for 300 ms after one closes, so scanning across meetings opens the next card at once. It also exports `noteHoverCardOpened`, `noteHoverCardClosed` and `resetHoverCardDelay` (for tests).
- **`OutlookMeetingSummary.vue`**: renders the eyebrow (dismissed variant included), the subject (`text-sm` 600, `line-clamp-3`, optional `subjectId`), the time row (the midnight-crossing rule moved here from the panel), the organizer row, the online row and a `meta` slot. Test ids are `<prefix>-subject|time|organizer|online`. It emits `subject-mounted` so the panel keeps labelling its dialog by the subject heading. Text is rendered only through interpolation.
- **`OutlookMeetingHoverCard.vue`**: a thin fork wrapper around `HoverCardPortal` > `HoverCardContent`: side right, align start, offset 8, avoid-collisions, `w-72 p-4`, PopoverContent styling, no role, `data-testid="outlook-hover-card"`. It contains the summary (prefix `outlook-hover`), the `hover_hint` line and an `actions` slot for Phase 4.
- **`OutlookGhostLayer.vue`**:
  - Each block now sits in `HoverCardRoot` (controlled) > `OutlookGhostDetail` (PopoverTrigger as-child) > `HoverCardTrigger as-child` > block div. The block keeps all of its existing attributes and the `data-panel-open` ring.
  - At most one `hoverId` per layer. reka's open requests are accepted only while a non-touch pointer rests on that block and its panel is closed. Keyboard focus and the focus that follows a click therefore never open a card.
  - Optional `contextMenuOpen` / `interacting` props suppress the card through its controlled `open`. A capture-phase `contextmenu` listener on the lane clears the panel and the card before reka's menu handler runs, after its nextTick. A watcher on `contextMenuOpen` does the same for right-clicks anywhere else. `setOpen` is ignored while the menu is open.
  - `interacting`, a zoom step (a watcher on `minutesToPixels(60)`) and a wheel inside `.fc` (passive capture listener on `document`) close the card. Unmounting the layer also closes it.
- **`OutlookGhostDetail.vue`**: renders its rows through the shared summary. The link, Separator, Dismiss/Restore, the `actions` and `footer-note` slots and every `outlook-detail-*` id are unchanged.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Hover card had no positioning anchor in the nested-trigger structure**
- **Found during:** Task 1, while reading the reka source.
- **Issue:** `HoverCardTrigger` registers its anchor through `PopperAnchor`, and `PopperAnchor` injects the *nearest* `PopperRoot` context. Inside `OutlookGhostDetail` the nearest one is the Popover's, so the HoverCard's own `PopperRoot` never received an anchor and the card could not be positioned next to the block.
- **Fix:** `OutlookMeetingHoverCard` takes a `reference` prop, which it passes to `HoverCardContent` (a PopperContent `reference`). The layer passes the block element, captured when the card opens. The trigger and grace-area wiring are unchanged, because `HoverCardRoot` context injection is not affected.
- **Files:** OutlookMeetingHoverCard.vue, OutlookGhostLayer.vue. **Commit:** d9c450e1.

**2. [Rule 2 - Design] Instant window also covers a card that is still open**
- The plan specifies the 300 ms window only after a card *closes*. reka closes card A only 120 ms after the pointer leaves, so moving straight from A to an adjacent B would otherwise still wait 400 ms. `hoverOpenDelay` is therefore also 0 while a card is open: B replaces A in the same layer, and A's late close request is ignored. This adds the extra export `noteHoverCardOpened`.
