# Phase 3.1 Plan 07: Browser proofs for the hover card, touch and context-menu layering Summary

`e2e/outlook-ghosts.spec.ts` now covers UX-03 and UX-04 in a real browser: the hover card details and its swap for the panel on click, a touch tap that opens only the panel, and the right-click menu staying on top of a hover card or panel (checked with `elementFromPoint`). It also checks the empty-lane and entry menu branches, that no card opens under an open menu, and that "Open in Outlook" opens a tab with `window.opener === null`.

## What was built

- **Helpers** (existing tests unchanged):
  - `LABELS` gains `hover_hint` (from a `HOVER_HINT` constant).
  - `InjectedMeeting` accepts optional `web_link`, `is_online_meeting` and `organizer_name`. `injectMeetings` now spreads them over the defaults.
  - New selectors `MEETING_MENU`, `HOVER_CARD`, `DETAIL_PANEL`.
  - `menuCentreHitsMenu(page)` reads the menu's bounding box and checks that `document.elementFromPoint` at its centre is inside the `[role="menu"]` that contains `outlook-meeting-menu`.
- **describe 'Outlook meeting hover card and context menu'**:
  1. *right-click over an open hover card closes it and opens the meeting menu on top*: hover shows the card (subject and "Click for details"). A right-click on the same block then removes the card and opens the meeting menu, which shows:
     - the subject as its label and "Open in Outlook";
     - no "Create Time Entry" and no Dismiss (test-hook mode).

     The centre hit-test passes and Escape closes the menu.
  2. *hovering shows the details; clicking swaps the card for the panel*: the card shows the eyebrow, `outlook-hover-subject`, `outlook-hover-time` (contains 14:00), `outlook-hover-online` and the hint. A click removes the card and shows `outlook-ghost-detail`.
  3. *with the panel open, a right-click closes it and Open in Outlook is usable*:
     - A context route answers `https://outlook.example.test/**` with a stub page.
     - Click opens the panel. A right-click removes the panel and opens the menu, and the centre hit-test passes.
     - Clicking `outlook-menu-open` (Playwright hit-tests it) opens a page at `https://outlook.example.test/meeting-1` whose `window.opener` is null (T-03.1-16).
  4. *right-click on empty lane space opens the empty-slot menu*: right-click at the lane's horizontal centre on the 16:00 row, after checking that the point is not on a meeting. "Create Time Entry" appears and no meeting menu.
  5. *right-click on an entry still opens the entry menu*: an API-created entry, plus a meeting at the same time. "Edit" appears and no meeting menu.
  6. *no hover card opens while the menu is open*: right-click the 14:00 meeting, move the pointer onto the 15:00 meeting and wait 700 ms. There is no hover card and the menu is still open.
- **describe 'Outlook meetings on touch'** (`test.use({ hasTouch: true })`, Firefox skipped): *a tap opens the panel and never the hover card*. After 700 ms there is still no card.

## Risks for the CI run

- The tests rely on 03.1-05's controlled hover card and the capture-phase `contextmenu` close. If CI shows the card or panel still mounted after a right-click, that is product behaviour to fix in the layer, not in the spec.
- The touch test assumes a Chromium touch tap produces a `click` on the block (reka PopoverTrigger). A failure there points at a pointerdown handler that suppresses the synthesized click.
