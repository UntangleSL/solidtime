## Phase Goal

**As a** team member, **I want** a calendar that is easier to read and move around (view options, readable meeting blocks, hover details, smooth zoom and scroll), **so that** working with time entries and Outlook meetings feels as fluid as in Clockify.

<objective>
UX-05 / D-07: predictable wheel and trackpad navigation: plain wheel scrolls, Ctrl/Cmd + wheel and pinch zoom the hour height in steps with the time under the pointer anchored, plus visible zoom buttons and a zoom pill. Zoom maps onto the existing Grid Scale (slotMinutes) ladder with a constant SLOT_HEIGHT (03.1-UI-SPEC section 6, checker revision 1; this SUPERSEDES the 03.1-RESEARCH slotHeight recommendation, whose wheel, accumulator, anchoring and pitfall guidance still applies).

Purpose: moving around the calendar feels like Clockify, without breaking drag, resize or selection snapping and without refetching on every notch.
Output: pure zoom helpers, wheel composable, corner controls, TimeEntryCalendar wiring (ledgered), Vitest and Playwright proof.
</objective>
