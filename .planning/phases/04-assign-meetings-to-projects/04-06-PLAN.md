## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** turn one or several meeting suggestions into time entries on a project in a single action, **so that** logging meeting time takes one click instead of re-typing it from Outlook.

<objective>
Keep the lane honest after entries change: refresh the meetings read (debounced) when the page's time entries refetch, so a deleted converted entry brings its meeting back; carry the linked entry ids from the server to the context for the Outlook icon; and let browser tests drive conversions through the test hook.

Purpose: ASGN-05 in the client (D-04 data, D-05) and the test-hook extension that gives 04-08 real Playwright coverage instead of manual UAT.
Output: meetingsApi parsing, context `linkedTimeEntryIds`, the refresh subscription, the hook's third argument, Vitest coverage.
</objective>
