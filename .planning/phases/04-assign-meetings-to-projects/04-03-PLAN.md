## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** turn one or several meeting suggestions into time entries on a project in a single action, **so that** logging meeting time takes one click instead of re-typing it from Outlook.

<objective>
Single conversion in the browser, end to end: the detail panel's "Create time entry" opens the mini form in place, the form is prefilled (subject, times, last project, billable), submits to the convert endpoint and the meeting leaves the lane while the new entry appears. Around it, the shared context gains the members the other wave-2 plans need: conversion, announcements, a request channel that opens a meeting's panel in form mode, and the multi-select state.

Purpose: ASGN-01 in the UI and the client half of ASGN-03 (D-01 panel, D-02, D-03, D-09, D-11, D-13).
Output: types, API helper, last-project helper, selection composable, context extensions, OutlookConvertForm (single mode), panel form mode, layer wiring, Vitest coverage.
</objective>
