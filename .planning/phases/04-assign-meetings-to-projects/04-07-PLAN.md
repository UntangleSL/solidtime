## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** turn one or several meeting suggestions into time entries on a project in a single action, **so that** logging meeting time takes one click instead of re-typing it from Outlook.

<objective>
Show where an entry came from: a small Outlook-origin icon on entries created from meetings, delivered through a minimal, ledgered core slot chain (the same seam style as the existing `day-overlay` slot), and the Phase 4 section of the core-file ledger.

Purpose: D-04 (converted meetings leave the lane and their entries show an Outlook icon) and the visible half of ASGN-05 (entries stay normal and editable). Uses the slot chain per the orchestrator's resolution 2 (the UI-SPEC suggested an `isFromMeeting` prop; a slot keeps the marker fork-owned and mirrors `day-overlay`).
Output: three additive core slot edits, one slot fill in Calendar.vue, OutlookEntryBadge, Vitest coverage, ledger section.
</objective>
