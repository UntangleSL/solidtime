## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** turn one or several meeting suggestions into time entries on a project in a single action, **so that** logging meeting time takes one click instead of re-typing it from Outlook.

<objective>
Every way into a conversion from a meeting block: "Create time entry" in the context menu and the hover card (both open the panel's form, D-17), and selecting several meetings with Ctrl/Cmd-click, Shift-click, the keyboard or the menu, with a clear selected look.

Purpose: roadmap criterion 6 (action on hover card and panel; the panel itself shipped in 04-03) and the input side of ASGN-04 (D-01, D-07, D-17). The selection bar, batch form and results are 04-05.
Output: menu items, hover-card action and hint, lane multi-select, Vitest coverage.
</objective>
