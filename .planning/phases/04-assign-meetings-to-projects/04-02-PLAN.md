## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** turn one or several meeting suggestions into time entries on a project in a single action, **so that** logging meeting time takes one click instead of re-typing it from Outlook.

<objective>
Make conversions safe to repeat and reversible: idempotent answers for repeats and races, per-meeting batch results, stale-link cleanup, proof that every core delete path brings the meeting back, links that survive re-sync and disconnect, the linked entry ids the calendar needs for the Outlook icon, and the impersonation and audit rules for the new data.

Purpose: ASGN-03, ASGN-04 and ASGN-05 on the server (D-05, D-06, D-08, D-13, D-15, D-16).
Output: controller changes, read-side extension, lifecycle and privacy tests; wave-2 CI gate (this plan is the wave-2 anchor).
</objective>
