## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Complete the contract lifecycle on the server: the summary lists and selects periods, account managers can change the terms of a contract that has already started (for this period and the following ones, or from the next period on, D-04 and UI-D9) without ever touching a closed period (D-03), and the materializer's lifecycle (gaps, end date, archive, resume) is proven.

Purpose: BUDG-01 edits, BUDG-02 renewal rules, BUDG-04 period selection; the second half of the 08-04 contract model.
Output: extended terms and summary controllers, model relations, one feature test class.
</objective>
