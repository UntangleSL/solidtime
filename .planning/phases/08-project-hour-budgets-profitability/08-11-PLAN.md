## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Serve the by-person view: each person's chargeability against their target over calendar-aligned ranges in the organization timezone (D-13), with absence time lowering available hours (D-06), for Owner and Admin only (D-16). The pure range resolver created here is reused by the Clients view (08-14).

Purpose: CHRG-02 formula and CHRG-03 data for the People tab.
Output: two pure helpers with unit tests, a read-only query, a service, an endpoint in its own route file, one feature test class.
</objective>
