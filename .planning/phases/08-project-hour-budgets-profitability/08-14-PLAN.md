## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Serve the by-client view: the account manager's portfolio (money sold, cost, margin and hours of their clients' contract projects, D-07, D-11) over calendar-aligned ranges, with straight-line monthly revenue (D-19) and the dated hourly cost (D-08); Owner and Admin see every client and the "No client" bucket.

Purpose: BUDG-06 client roll-up for the Clients tab (08-17).
Output: one consumption method, one service, one endpoint, one feature test class.
</objective>
