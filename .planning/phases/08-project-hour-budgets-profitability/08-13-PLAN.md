## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Grow the 08-06 Contract card on the project page into the full summary of the UI-SPEC: period selection, hours KPIs with the projection to close, the budget meter, the planned-vs-consumed chart with its month table, the money strip for scopes with money, and the states of closed, upcoming, over-budget, archived and incomplete-cost periods.

Purpose: D-02's key output ("within the hours sold, how much has been consumed and how much must still be consumed by the period close") on each project page (D-14), BUDG-04 and the money half of BUDG-06 for the project.
Output: the full card, a chart component with a pure option builder, a month table, the extended parser.
</objective>
