## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Let the project manager rebalance a period's hours across its months (D-02, BUDG-04 write side) under the sum-equals-hours-sold invariant, and close the renewal lifecycle: a daily safety-net command renews periods (D-04), and archiving or un-archiving a project pauses and resumes renewal without back-filling (D-18).

Purpose: the PM's planning action and the scheduler/archive behaviour of BUDG-02 that the read-time materializer (08-04, 08-08) relies on.
Output: plan endpoint, renewal command and schedule, archive listener, two feature test classes.
</objective>
