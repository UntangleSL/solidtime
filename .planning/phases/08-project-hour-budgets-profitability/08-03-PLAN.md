## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Turn project manager and account manager into assignments (D-10) and make the single access resolver honour them (D-11, D-16): a project manager sees hour balances of the projects they manage without money, an account manager sees their clients' projects with money and may edit terms, a core Manager without assignment sees nothing. Owner and Admin manage the assignments through settings endpoints.

Purpose: BUDG-07 (assignments, no new core roles) and the access half of BUDG-06 (money only for account managers of the client and Owner/Admin).
Output: assignment tables and models, the completed resolver (interface unchanged from 08-01), assignment endpoints, three test classes.
</objective>
