## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Finish the tracer at the browser: the Contract card on each project page shows the current period's hours sold and consumed from the 08-01 endpoint, using the 08-02 shared prop for its URL and copy, mounted in ProjectShow.vue with one import and one element. The shared frontend building blocks of the phase come with it: the formatters, the label helper, the status pill and the budget meter.

Purpose: D-14 (summary on each project's page) and the browser end of BUDG-03; the formatters and the two visual primitives every later view reuses (UI-D1, UI-D2, UI-D6).
Output: format and label helpers, the summary client, the card and its core seam, StatusPill and BudgetMeter, with Vitest specs.
</objective>
