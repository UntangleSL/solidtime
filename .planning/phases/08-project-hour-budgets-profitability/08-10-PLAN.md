## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Add money and the projection to the period balance and expose the Projects view: cost with the dated hourly cost per person (D-08) through one SQL lateral join, revenue spread evenly per month (D-19), margin to date and projected, all merged only for scopes with money (D-07, D-11); the pure projection to close (remaining plan and run rate, D-02) with a status; and the by-project list behind the Profitability page (D-14), including the legacy non-billable notice for Owner/Admin (D-05).

Purpose: BUDG-04 projection, BUDG-05 margins with dated cost, BUDG-06 money redaction on the server, BUDG-03 timezone-correct bucketing.
Output: extended consumption query, projection classes, the balance service, the extended summary, the Projects view endpoint, four test classes.
</objective>
