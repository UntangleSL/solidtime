## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Store the per-person data that margins and chargeability need: dated hourly cost (D-08, BUDG-05), dated working week with chargeability target (D-13, CHRG-01), and the explicit absence-project markers for Vacaciones and Festivo (D-06, CHRG-02 input). Only Owner and Admin read or write any of it (D-12, D-16).

Purpose: inputs for the margin (08-10) and chargeability (08-11) calculations, behind the strictest scope.
Output: one migration with three tables, three models, two controllers, three requests, one route file, two test classes.
</objective>
