## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Give account managers the contract terms editor (BUDG-01, D-01, D-12, D-15, D-04 apply-to) and project managers the monthly plan editor (BUDG-04, D-02), reachable from the Contract card and from the Projects view, so the whole contract workflow runs in the browser.

Purpose: the user-facing write side of the contract model; the server rules already exist (08-04, 08-08, 08-09).
Output: two dialogs, a client mirror of the plan split, an API module, and the wiring into the card and the Projects view.
</objective>
