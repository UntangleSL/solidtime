## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Give a project its contract terms and its periods (BUDG-01, BUDG-02): one PUT saves the terms of a project that has none, a lazy and idempotent materializer turns them into contract periods (stub first period per D-15, renewing monthly, quarterly or annually, clean each time per D-03) with an even monthly plan (D-02), and the summary exposes the period's months with planned and consumed hours (BUDG-04 read side). Editing terms after a contract has started, the period list and the materializer lifecycle tests follow in 08-08.

Purpose: the contract model of D-01..D-04 and D-15 on the server, with the period arithmetic in pure classes proven without a database.
Output: the schedule enum, four period classes, the terms endpoint, the months in the summary, three test classes.
</objective>
