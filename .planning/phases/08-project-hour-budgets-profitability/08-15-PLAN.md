## Phase Goal

**As** Untangle management, **I want** each client project to carry its contract terms (hours and money sold, renewing on a schedule), the team's billable time to consume them, and a view of profitability and team chargeability, **so that** we see per month, per project, per client and per person whether we are on track to the contract close.

<objective>
Give Owner and Admin the Settings tab (D-12, D-16): people with dated hourly cost and dated working week plus target (BUDG-05, CHRG-01), project and account manager assignments (BUDG-07, D-10) and the absence projects (D-06).

Purpose: the configuration the margins and chargeability depend on, in the browser, without any developer step.
Output: settings API module, the Settings view with three cards and the person dialog, and the page map entry.
</objective>
