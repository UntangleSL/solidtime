## Phase Goal

As Untangle, I want solidtime to look like our own tool by default and let each user tune it, so that the app feels branded and comfortable to use.

<objective>
Apply the user's theme and accent before the first paint on every device: one fork Blade partial, included in the Inertia root view and in the OAuth authorize page (replacing its inline copy), injects the session user's stored values and runs a small synchronous script that sets the classes on `<html>`, with localStorage and the defaults as fallback.

Purpose: BRAND-02 "the choice persists" without a flash of the wrong theme or accent, and BRAND-01 on the OAuth page too (D-01, D-02, D-05).
Output: the partial, two layout edits, a PHPUnit rendering test and a Vitest test that executes the shipped script.
</objective>
