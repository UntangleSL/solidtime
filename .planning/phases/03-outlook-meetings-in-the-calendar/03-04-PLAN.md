## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
The user-visible tracer of the phase: opening `/calendar` shows the user's cached Outlook meetings as read-only ghost blocks in the week and day views, at the correct local times, without blocking time entries, without ever starting a time-entry selection, drag or resize, and without touching totals. The ghost layer is a fork component injected through two new slots, so the core calendar changes by a handful of ledgered lines (03-RESEARCH Pattern 3; 03-UI-SPEC section 1).

Purpose: CAL-01, CAL-02, SYNC-01 (client side), SYNC-02 (debounce and single flight) and SYNC-04 (display) become visible to the user.
Output: calendar shared prop and copy, query composable, layout helper, ghost layer, core slot seams, test hook, Vitest and Playwright proof, ledger rows; CI green.
</objective>
