## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
The Sync half of the Outlook control cluster in the calendar toolbar (03-UI-SPEC section 3 and section 5): a "Sync Outlook" button with cooldown and back-off awareness and a quiet status line (syncing, last synced, empty, unavailable, no mailbox), injected through one ledgered toolbar slot. The Connect/Reconnect variants and their return to the calendar follow in 03-08.

Purpose: SYNC-02 (manual Sync without hammering Graph) and SYNC-05 (non-blocking status) become usable from the calendar.
Output: toolbar slot seam, control component, status helper, composable extensions (provide/inject, cooldown, connection-state override), Vitest proof; CI green. No backend change: the copy was shared by 03-06.
</objective>
