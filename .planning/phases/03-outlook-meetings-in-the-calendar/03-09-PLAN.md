## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
Give every ghost a detail panel and let the user dismiss a suggestion (CAL-04, D-04, D-07, D-20), with the backend for both directions (dismiss, restore, include_dismissed). The panel is built once as an extendable component so Phase 4 ("Assign to project") and Phase 5 (series actions) only fill its slots. Dismissal is per occurrence, persisted on the cached row and preserved by every re-sync. Showing dismissed meetings again and restoring them from the calendar follow in 03-10.

Purpose: the user decides what the calendar suggests, and nothing is lost by a click.
Output: dismiss/restore endpoints, include_dismissed read, panel component, optimistic dismiss with revert, announcements and focus, PHP/Vitest/Playwright proof; CI green.
</objective>
