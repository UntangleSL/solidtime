## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
Make the sync safe under pressure and failure (03-RESEARCH Pattern 4, Error Taxonomy, Pitfalls 2, 3, 8, 9): Outlook throttling is honoured with a persisted back-off from `Retry-After` (D-12); repeated presses and fast navigation are absorbed by a 60 s reuse window (D-11), a 10 s minimum interval for forced syncs and a per-user lock; outages, timeouts and the "no mailbox" answer degrade to typed statuses while the cache stays readable; and the cache reconciles moved, cancelled and removed meetings only after a complete traversal, scrubbing anything that turned ineligible.

Purpose: SYNC-02 and SYNC-05 on the server, plus the round A to round B behaviour of SYNC-03, before the UI exposes a Sync button.
Output: RetryAfter helper, client error mapping and deadline, sync-service guards and reconcile, `sync.retry_after` on the read endpoint, resilience tests green in CI.
</objective>
