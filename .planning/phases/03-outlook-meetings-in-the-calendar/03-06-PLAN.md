## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
Give every ghost a detail panel and let the user dismiss a suggestion and bring it back (CAL-04, D-04, D-07, D-08, D-20). The panel is built once as an extendable component so Phase 4 ("Assign to project") and Phase 5 (series actions) only fill its slots. Dismissal is per occurrence, persisted on the cached row, preserved by every re-sync, reversible through "Show dismissed meetings" in the calendar settings.

Purpose: the user decides what the calendar suggests; nothing is hidden for them and nothing is lost by a click.
Output: dismiss/restore endpoints, include_dismissed read, panel component, dismissed variant, settings switch through a ledgered slot chain, Vitest/PHP/Playwright proof; CI green.
</objective>
