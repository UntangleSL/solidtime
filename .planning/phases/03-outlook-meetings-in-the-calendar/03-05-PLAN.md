## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
The ghost layer of the calendar as a self-contained frontend slice: a fork component rendered through a new overlay slot of the core day column, laid out in its own reserved right-hand "Outlook lane" so that a meeting is always visible and reachable even when the user already logged an entry at the same time (D-01, layout decision D-23), and inert for every time-entry interaction (selection, drag, resize, context of totals). Positions are DST-correct in the user's timezone. Data wiring (shared prop, query, sync) follows in 03-06.

Purpose: CAL-01, CAL-02 and the display half of SYNC-04, with the D-01 reachability rule proven by Vitest geometry and pointer tests.
Output: types and labels helper, layout helper, ghost layer component, three minimal ledgered core seams, Vitest proof; CI green.
</objective>
