## Phase Goal

**As a** team member tracking time, **I want to** see my Outlook meetings as suggestions in the solidtime calendar, **so that** I can see which meetings still need logging without switching to Outlook.

<objective>
Complete CAL-04 (D-08): the user can show dismissed suggestions again from the calendar settings and restore them. The switch lives in a new `settings-extra` slot of the core settings popover, forwarded through the toolbar and the calendar so the upstream popover is unchanged without fork content; dismissed ghosts get a distinct variant with a Restore action.

Purpose: dismissing is always reversible from the calendar itself.
Output: settings slot chain, setting component, include_dismissed read, restore with rollback and announcement, dismissed variant, Vitest proof; CI green.
</objective>
